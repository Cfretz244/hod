# WP-220 — AVX-512 step 4: VBMI, GFNI, VAES, VPCLMULQDQ, BF16, FP16

Design: `docs/design/0007-avx512.md` (follow-ups A and B of section
5.2; the table of section 5.1 has each family's state). Spec:
`spec/codegen.md` §5.6.35 items (4) to (10), `spec/mc-x64.md` "EVEX and
the opmask instructions".

3. **66 intrinsic names** (`hod_ir` `Intrinsic` members, matrix rows
   `never(X64_ONLY)` / `on(X86_SSE)`, notes `AVX-512 (WP-220)`,
   `VAES (WP-220)`, `VPCLMULQDQ (WP-220)`):
   - VBMI, 9: `avx512.permvar.qi`, `vpermi2var.qi`, `pmultishift.qb`,
     each at `.128`, `.256`, `.512` (§5.6.35 (4));
   - GFNI, 2: `vgf2p8affineqb.512`, `vgf2p8mulb.512` (5);
   - VAES, 8: `aesni.aes{enc,enclast,dec,declast}.512` (EVEX) and
     `.256` (VEX, `x86::ext`) (6);
   - VPCLMULQDQ, 2: `pclmulqdq.512` (EVEX), `pclmulqdq.256` (VEX) (7);
   - BF16, 6: `avx512bf16.cvtne2ps2bf16.{128,256,512}`,
     `avx512bf16.cvtneps2bf16.512`, `vcvtneps2bf16128`, `vcvtneps2bf16256`

     `mask.rsqrt.ph`, `mask.rndscale.ph`, `mask.vcvtpd2ph`,
     `mask.vcvtph2pd`, `mask.vcvtph2w`, `mask.vcvttph2w`,
     `mask.vcvttph2uw`, `max.ph`, `min.ph`, `vfmaddsub.ph`, each at

   The 61 EVEX names are rows of `crates/hod_x64/src/x86/avx512.rs` on
   WP-208's machinery (operands through `zmm16` up, the mask in `k1`,
   rounding and immediates as there). New in it: `i8` immediates
   (`vgf2p8affineqb`, `vpclmulqdq`), `half` and `bfloat` lanes taken as
   16-bit lanes, `vfpclassph` of 32 lanes moving `k0` with `kmovd`.

## Refused by name

- The 16 F gathers and scatters of the census
  (`intrinsic:llvm.x86.avx512.mask.gather.{dpi,dps,qpd,qpq}.512`,
  `mask.scatter.{dpi,dps,qpd,qpq}.512`, `mask.scatterdiv2.{df,di}`,
  `mask.scatterdiv4.{df,di}`, `mask.scattersiv4.{sf,si}`,
  `mask.scattersiv8.{sf,si}`). Not started: they need the EVEX VSIB
  operand in the encoder (`V'` as the index's bit 4, its own L1 sweep),
  lowering that lets the instruction clear its opmask, and a dataflow
  interpreter that follows memory reached through a vector of indices.
  None of that machinery exists, so the evidence this package demands
  could not be had within it. They are the next AVX-512 step.
- Each WP-220 name without its family (above); each EVEX one with
  `-avx512vl` below 512 bits (an FP16 one at every length, FP16 needing
  VL), with `-evex512` at 512, and GFNI, VAES and VPCLMULQDQ at 512
  without `avx512f`; a rounding operand outside its set
  (`x64:x86-intrinsic-signature`); `vcvtneps2bf16128`/`256` under
  AVX-NE-CONVERT alone; a `mask.compress` of `half` or `bfloat` lanes
  (`x64:x86-intrinsic-signature`).

## The `vgf2p8affineqb` broadcast displacement

`llvm-mc` 19 and 22 disagree on the compressed `disp8` of
`vgf2p8affineqb` with a broadcast operand. For
`vgf2p8affineqb zmm31, zmm8, qword ptr [r12 - 64]{1to8}, 7`, measured:
`llvm-mc` 22 `62 43 bd 58 ce 7c 24 f8 07` (`disp8` -8: `N` 8, the SDM's
Full tuple with `W1` broadcast), `llvm-mc` 19 `62 43 bd 58 ce 7c 24 c0 07`
(`disp8` -64: `N` 1). hod follows 22 and the SDM (a unit test in
`evex.rs` pins those bytes); the L1 sweep keeps to displacements both
versions encode alike (0, and 0x408, which neither compresses). hod's
lowering writes no broadcast operand, so no object hod writes depends
on the disagreement.

## Census

The second real-project lane's saved modules of highway's default
x86-64 build (`.build/findings/oss2/x64/highway`, outside the
repository): one directory per name main `3b14f8ff` refuses, 81 names,
in 23 distinct modules (8 unreduced, of 24 to 240 MB, shared between
names; 15 reduced by `llvm-reduce`). hod `--strict -O0` over each
distinct module, base `3b14f8ff` and this branch:

| | base | this branch |
|---|---|---|
| distinct names refused | 81 | 31 |
| modules accepted whole | 0 of 23 | 7 of 23 |

The 7 are all the unreduced modules but `memory_test.cc`'s (from
`combine_test.cc`, `convert_test.cc`, `float_test.cc`, `fma_test.cc`,
`highway_test.cc`, `shift_test.cc`, `table_test.cc`). Left, each in 1
module:

- the 16 gathers and scatters, all in `memory_test.cc`'s module, which
  refuses nothing else;
- 15 names whose only module is a reduced one: `aesni.aes{enc,enclast,
  dec,declast}.{256,512}`, `pclmulqdq.{256,512}`, `vgf2p8affineqb.512`
  (`crypto_test.cc`), `avx512.permvar.qi.512` (`table_test.cc`),
  `avx512bf16.cvtne2ps2bf16.512` (`highway_test.cc`),
  `avx512bf16.cvtneps2bf16.512` (`combine_test.cc`),
  `avx512fp16.mask.vcvtpd2ph.512` (`convert_test.cc`). Each reduced
  function has lost its `target-features`, so the family is off and the
  name is refused, rightly. With highway's AVX3_SPR feature string (taken
  from the unreduced `shift_test.cc` module) put back on the function,
  this branch accepts 14 of the 15 and base refuses all 15;
  `vcvtpd2ph.512` is then `x64:x86-intrinsic-signature`, because the
  reduction turned its rounding operand 4 into 0, which `llc` 19 and 22
  cannot select either; with 4 restored it is accepted. Five of the 15
  are also called in an unreduced module this branch accepts.

So every census name but the 16 gathers and scatters is built. By the
saved modules, of the objects the lane saw declined for these names,
all but `memory_test.cc`'s refuse only names this branch builds; the
`.cc` files whose modules are no longer saved are inferred from the
lane's earlier census, not run.

## Evidence

**The encoder** (L1 against `llvm-mc` 19 and 22, each the same):
`evex` 35,981 encodings (1,830 rejections, 604 of them confirmed by
`llvm-mc`), `evex-memory-operands` 21,397, `vex` 18,858 (18
rejections, 9 confirmed), `vex-gathers` 2,432.

**The lowering, by dataflow** (`avx512_names_agree_with_llc_by_dataflow`):
244 names (WP-208's 174, the 61 EVEX names, the 5 VEX names and the
128- and 256-bit GFNI ones the interpreter now reads), 1,080 functions,
930 of them computing a result; every one equals `llc -O0` 22's or 19's
under hod plain and under every stress option: 0 disagreements in both
legs. The interpreter reads a quarter of `vcvtph2pd`'s source and folds
a masked move of a whole unmasked node into that node masked (`llc`
keeps `vcvtph2pd.512` and its mask apart). Mutations of `llc` 22's
listings (`dataflow_comparison_catches_mutations`), every one caught:
swapped sources 276 of 276, another immediate 329 of 329, merging
instead of zeroing 106 of 106, zeroing instead of merging 182 of 182,
no mask 394 of 394, another mask register 479 of 479, another rounding
9 of 9, no rounding 46 of 46, a narrower store 372 of 372, a source from
another slot 377 of 377. EVEX locals keep their `disp32`
(`evex_frame_locals_keep_their_displacement`): 3,532 EVEX accesses,
2,235 within a byte of `rbp`.

**Refusals** (`avx512_refusals_and_rows`): 244 rows, each accepted with
its family and refused by name without it as listed above (the VEX
forms accepted without `avx512f`); `features::tests` and
`x86_ext::only_with_the_feature` for the gates of (2).

**Execution.** The 256-bit VAES forms run under `qemu-x86_64 -cpu max`
in `x86_ext` (178 intrinsics, 895 functions, 21,480 cases; legs hod
plain with bfd and lld, hod stressed, `llc` 22 and 19, all agree; 648
cases skipped where QEMU's model lacks a feature, which are
`pclmulqdq.256` and the GFNI forms: it has neither GFNI nor
VPCLMULQDQ). A mutation of `aesenc.256` to `vaesdec` was caught when
the rows were written (not rerun for this note). **No EVEX name of this
package executed**: no emulator here runs AVX-512. The execution test
links its 974 functions and runs them only with
`HOD_X64_AVX512_RUNNER`, on an AVX-512 host:
`HOD_REQUIRE_TOOLS=1 HOD_X64_AVX512_RUNNER=native cargo test -p hod_x64 --test x86_avx512`.

**Byte identity**, every 10th entry of each corpus
(`scripts/corpus-bytes.py -j 4`), base `3b14f8ff` against this branch:
AArch64 (`.build/corpus`) 669 modules, 669 objects identical, 0
declines, 0 differ; x86-64 (`.build/corpus-x64`) 684 modules, 684
objects identical, 0 declines, 0 differ.

**Tests** at the branch head: `cargo fmt --all --check`,
