# WP-208 — AVX-512

Design: `docs/design/0007-avx512.md`. Spec: `spec/codegen.md` §5.6.35,
`spec/mc-x64.md` "EVEX and the opmask instructions".

## Landed

1. **The EVEX encoder and the opmask instructions**
   (`crates/hod_mc_x64/src/evex.rs`): the prefix, compressed `disp8*N`,
   masking, broadcast, `{er}`/`{sae}`, the families the F/VL, BW and DQ
   names need, `kmov`/`k*` logic, tests and shifts. L1
   (`crates/hod_mc_x64/tests/l1/evex.rs`), against `llvm-mc` 19 and 22:
   group `evex` 30,424 encodings (1,397 rejections, 378 of them confirmed
   by `llvm-mc`), `evex-memory-operands` 17,770, `opmask` 2,328.
2. **The masked memory intrinsics** (`llvm.masked.load`, `.store`,
   `.expandload`, `.compressstore`; `crates/hod_x64/src/vector.rs`
   `masked`, reached from `x86::intrinsic`; four `hod_ir` `Intrinsic`
   members after the x86 ones, matrix rows at the end of
   `INTRINSIC_X86`, `todo(LATER)` / `on(MASKED)`), lane by lane on every
   x86-64 function, §5.6.35 (1).
3. **The AVX-512 F, VL, BW and DQ names of the census** and the VBMI2
   compresses (`crates/hod_x64/src/x86/avx512.rs`, reached from
   `x86::intrinsic` after `vector::masked`; 164 `hod_ir` `Intrinsic`
   members, matrix rows `never(X64_ONLY)` / `on(X86_SSE)`, note
   `AVX-512 (WP-208)`; the family gates `features::Avx512` and
   `features::evex512_off`), §5.6.35 (2).
4. **EVEX accesses to locals keep their `disp32`**
   (`crates/hod_x64/src/common.rs` `frame_inst`, `bias_local_sites`):
   the frame layout's `disp8` shortening (§5.6.17) skips a field whose
   instruction starts with the EVEX escape `62`, since EVEX scales a
   `disp8` by `N`. EVEX sites are excluded rather than shortened to a
   scaled `disp8`, so no other instruction's bytes change. `avx512.rs`
   addresses its slots directly (the `lea r11` it used before is gone).
5. **512-bit vectors and vectors of `i1` by value** (`spec/abi-x64.md`
   §1.3, §3.2, §4.3, §5.1, §8.7, §9.1; `spec/codegen.md` §5.6.35 (3)):
   `features::of_function_with` decides, as `llc`'s `useAVX512Regs`,
   whether a function's 512-bit vectors are legal (`avx512vl`, the
   preferred width from `prefer-128-bit`/`prefer-256-bit` or the tuning
   CPU's `TUNE_256`, `"min-legal-vector-width"`); `abi.rs` gains the
   class `Zmm` (33 to 64 bytes in one `zmm`, else two `ymm`; on the stack
   in a 64-byte slot aligned to 64; returned in `zmm0`..`zmm3`) and the
   masks under AVX-512 (32 elements in a `ymm`'s bytes, 64 in a `zmm`'s or
   two `ymm`'s with `avx512bw`, other lengths up to 64 a byte each);
   `vector::load_wide`/`store_wide` move 17 to 64 bytes (`vmovdqu64` for
   a `zmm`), `vcall.rs` moves the lanes through a slot of the register's
   size, `call.rs` aligns the argument area to 64. Refused: AVX-512 whose
   conventions are unknown (`-evex512`, AVX10, `amx-avx512`, an
   unreadable width) and a mask of more than 64 elements. The
   `musttail` thunk under `+avx512f` now compiles (`musttail_x64.rs`).

## Census

The second real-project lane's saved modules (WP-200): 27 distinct
modules, 54 distinct masked memory names (18 shapes each of `load`,
`store`, `compressstore`, LLVM 22's signatures); every one of the 18
shapes is a row of the execution test's `SHAPES`, so every census name is
built and executed. Refused names left, by family, in the design's
section 5.1.

## Evidence

**The masked memory intrinsics, by execution** (`masked_memory_intrinsics_agree_with_llc`):
148 functions (each intrinsic on 21 shapes, from `<2 x float>` to
`<64 x i8>`, `<3 x i32>` and `half` lanes among them, with a variable
mask; and with the constant masks none, all, alternating and the top
lane on four shapes), 8 cases each (masks 0, all ones and random; random
memory and pass-through), 1,184 cases. Legs: hod plain (bfd and lld),
hod under every stress option, hod from LLVM 22's spelling of the
signatures, hod in functions with AVX2; `llc` 22 and 19 without features
(scalarized) and with AVX2 (`vmaskmov`), `llc` 22 on LLVM 22's spelling.
All print the same bytes under `qemu-x86_64 -cpu max`. The memory a
call may touch ends at an inaccessible page, so a lane whose bit is
clear and that touched memory would fault (the `llc` AVX2 legs run
without that page: QEMU faults on `vmaskmov` lanes the hardware
suppresses).

Mutations (each run once, then reverted): the lane test inverted (`jb`
for `jae`), and the packed forms addressing lane `i` instead of the
`k`-th set lane: both fault on the inaccessible page in the hod legs.

**Byte identity.** Every 10th entry of the x86-64 corpus
(`.build/corpus-x64`, `scripts/corpus-bytes.py -j 4`), base `b5545a9`
against this branch: 684 modules, 682 objects identical, 2 declines
identical, 0 differ.

**The AVX-512 names, by dataflow** (`avx512_names_agree_with_llc_by_dataflow`):
174 names (the 164 members, `mask.compress` on its eleven shapes), 802
functions, 679 of them computing a result; every one equals `llc -O0`
22's or 19's under hod plain and under every stress option (0
disagreements in both legs). The interpreter catches every deliberate
change to `llc` 22's listings (`dataflow_comparison_catches_mutations`):
swapped sources 192 of 192, another immediate 232 of 232, merging
instead of zeroing 79 of 79, zeroing instead of merging 128 of 128, no
mask 286 of 286, another mask register 347 of 347, another rounding 6
of 6, no rounding 32 of 32, a narrower store 283 of 283, a source from
another slot 253 of 253. Refusals (`avx512_refusals_and_rows`), on
all 174 rows: accepted with every family on; refused by name with its
family off, with `-avx512vl` exactly below 64 bytes (but the `.sd`
rows), with `-evex512` exactly at 64 bytes; a rounding operand outside
its set is `x64:x86-intrinsic-signature`; the matrix row is supported on
x86-64 and not on AArch64. A wrong-length signature (`getexp.ps.512` on
`<8 x float>`) is `x64:x86-intrinsic-signature`. The
execution test links its 723 functions (the undefined pass-through cases
left out) and runs only with `HOD_X64_AVX512_RUNNER`, on an AVX-512 host:
`HOD_REQUIRE_TOOLS=1 HOD_X64_AVX512_RUNNER=native cargo test -p hod_x64 --test x86_avx512`.

**Byte identity, AVX-512 names.** Every 10th entry of the x86-64 corpus,
base `8fb9afa` against this branch: 684 modules, 682 objects identical, 2
declines identical, 0 differ.

**512-bit vectors and masks by value.** Nothing here executes AVX-512,
so the conventions are compared with `llc`'s MIR after instruction
selection and the bytes by dataflow:

- `abi::zmm_grid`: 886 signatures on each of 18 machines (AVX-512 F
  alone, with VL, BW and DQ, `prefer-256-bit` either way, `tune-cpu`,
  `min-legal-vector-width` 0, 256, 512 or absent, the census'
  `skylake-avx512`, `znver4`, `sapphirerapids`, `x86-64-v4`, and
  `knl`), 15,948 in all, 28 per machine refused as `x64:mask-vector`
  (masks over 64 elements). hod's convention of a definition receiving
  each and of a call making it equals `llc` 19's and 22's at `-O0`
  (`FastISel` and `SelectionDAG`): 111,338 conventions equal, 0 differ
  (6,201 per machine where 512-bit vectors are legal, 6,161 where they
  are not). Left out: LLVM 19 on a general value it keeps whole with
  something on the stack (§4.2, as the grid), and LLVM 19's `FastISel`
  on a `<32 x bfloat>`, which it drops from a call on `znver4` and
  compiles until memory runs out on `sapphirerapids`.
- `abi::wide_vector_grid` under `skylake-avx512`: 1,178 signatures,
  8,175 conventions equal to `llc`'s, 0 differ.
- `abi::conventions_tell_zmm_from_two_ymm`: hod's assignment for a

- `abi::clang_signatures_agree_with_their_llc`: C++ with `__m512`,
