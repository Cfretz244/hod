# WP-177 — real projects through kiln and hod (the L9 lane)

Landed: `scripts/oss/`, a port of kiln's open-source project harness
(kiln's `scripts/oss/`, its `spec/landed/harness/WP-666.md`), building
projects through their own build systems with kiln at `-O0` and
`-O0 -g`, three ways (kiln with `llc`, kiln with hod falling back, kiln
with hod strict), their tests compared with the control; the contract in
`spec/harness.md` "Real projects", the layer in `docs/VALIDATION.md`
"Real projects (L9)", the first numbers in `docs/status/oss.md`, and a
hosted workflow `.github/workflows/oss.yml` (written, not yet run).

## Decisions

- **The level.** kiln offers hod only `-O0` modules, and every row of
  kiln's list builds `Release`. The lane configures `Debug` with the
  flavour as the Debug flags and has the wrapper append the flavour's
  flags last (`-O0 -g0` or `-O0 -g`), so a row that pins a level (kiln
  names rocksdb's `-O1`) is still at `-O0`. The control gets the same
  flags: the comparison is between back ends only.
- **Configure with LLVM.** A configure-time compile hod declined would,
  in strict mode, fail a feature test and change what is built; so all
  three builds configure with `KILN_BACKEND=llvm` and differ from the
  build on.
- **Strict is the shim's.** kiln falls back from a decline (status 2)
  even under `KILN_HOD_STRICT=1` (kiln's `spec/driver.md` "Back-end
  selection": only a failure is an error); the shim answers 1 for 2
  under `HOD_OSS_STRICT=1`, which kiln turns into a compile error.
- **Ground truth.** The census reads kiln's statistics log
  (`KILN_STATS_LOG`) for what kiln offered and what came of it
  (`backend`), hod's coverage log for hod's own count and the census
  reasons, and the shim's log for the function of each refusal. A
  refusal hod reports without a function (`attr:<a>`) gets the functions
  whose attribute group holds `<a>`, found in the kept module.
- **The modules.** The shim keeps the exact text kiln piped to hod (no
  rerun with `-S -emit-llvm`), one per compiled source, at most three
  per reason per build; `collect` keeps at most three per reason per
  project, one per source.
- **Disk.** Every tree is pruned to its logs and report after its run,
  unless its tests do worse than the control's; dependency trees always.
- **Rows.** Only cmake rows: the first rows and the next ones in kiln's
  list are all cmake. zstd's row drops kiln's `playTests.sh` step (it
  runs under `/tmp`).

## Results

`docs/status/oss.md` (generated) has the numbers, on AArch64. With the
hod of wave 10, 12 rows built in both flavours fell back in five
(googletest, abseil, protobuf, yaml_cpp, glog) on eight refusal names
(`debug:variable-twice`, `debug:local-static`, `attr:disable-tail-calls`,
`debug:template-address`, `asm:operand/sysreg`,
`alwaysinline:return-address`, `alwaysinline:returns-twice`,
`debug:die-unsupported`); the hod of wave 12 (merged in) builds them again,
hybrid and strict. No test passes under `llc` and fails under hod; the
two candidates the lane flagged were a flaky spdlog test and a control
report written by the old ctest parser (an abort read as a pass).
