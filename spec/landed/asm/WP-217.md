# WP-217: alignment directives in AArch64 templates, scalar SIMD forms

Spec: `spec/asm.md` §17 (and §5.2, §5.3, §7, §8, §16);
`spec/codegen.md` §10 (`limit:aligned-code-moved`).

## What was built

- `crates/hod_asm/src/align.rs`: the operands of `.p2align`, `.balign`,
  `.align` (`n{, fill}{, max}`) as `llvm-mc` 19 and 22 read them, and the
  padding from an offset; module text (`module.rs`) reads them through
  it (without `max`).
- `crates/hod_asm/src/a64/mod.rs`: `assemble_at(body, features, start,
  ..)` pads to boundaries counted from `start` with `nop` words or the
  fill byte; `Code::align` is the body's largest alignment.
- `crates/hod_a64/src/inline_asm.rs`: a body that aligns is assembled
  again at its offset and the function's alignment raised
  (`FnCx::raise_align`, `crates/hod_cg/src/cx.rs`).
- `crates/hod_cg/src/shrink.rs`: bytes removed before an aligned
  template refuse the function (`limit:aligned-code-moved`); on AArch64
  a probed frame's prologue room is the one cut.
- `crates/hod_asm/src/a64/encode.rs`: scalar `fcvtzs`, `fcvtzu`,
  `fcmeq` (register, `#0.0`) on `s`/`d` registers.

## LLVM's behaviour, measured with llvm-mc and llc 19 and 22

The section offset is the origin, and the section's alignment is raised
to the directive's even when `max` skips the padding. Fill 0 in code is
`nop`s to 19 and zeros to 22 (hod: 22). `max` below 1 and `.balign 3`
are errors. Names are read in any case. On x86-64 the `nop` length
follows the function's tuning, and fill `0x90` differs between the
majors as fill 0 does here.

## Evidence

- `cargo test -q -p hod_asm --test llvm_mc`: the nine scalar bodies,
  equal to `llvm-mc` 22's and 19's words; 21 new refusals by name; 19
  aligned bodies (upb's jump table among them) at 16 start offsets each,
  equal to `llvm-mc` 22's and 19's words; fill 0 equal to 22's.
- `cargo test -q -p hod_a64 --test inline_asm_align`: 7 bodies in 4
  functions (28 templates, the first body met at several offsets
  modulo 16), with `-function-sections`. In hod's object and in `llc`
  19's and 22's, every template's bytes equal `llvm-mc`'s at its section
  offset, and every `.text.f<n>` is 64-aligned in all three. In one
  `.text`, the instruction after each directive is on its boundary in
  all three. A probed frame with an aligned template is
  `limit:aligned-code-moved`; without the probe it compiles.
- `cargo test -q -p hod_a64 --test inline_asm`: these seeds were built
  by hod (plain, stress, far) and by `llc` 19 and 22, linked with the
  driver under bfd, lld and mold, dynamic and static, and run natively.
  - `align_probe`: `adr` after `.p2align 5`, `.balign 64`, `.align 4`,
    and `.P2ALIGN 3, 0xff`.
  - `align_fill`.
  - `zstd_loop`: zstd's census templates, `.p2align 6`/`5`/`4`/`3`
    around a loop.
  - `upb_longvarint`: upb's `encode_longvarint` template, checked
    against a C model.

  Every check passes. `llc` 19's one failure is the known `goto_out2`
  failure, unrelated to this package.
- zstd (mold's copy, built by kiln `-O0` for aarch64):
  - `zstd_lazy` and `zstd_decompress_block` build with `hod --strict`
    and exit 0. They contain no inline-asm template, because zstd's
    `.p2align` templates are x86-64 only.
  - The x86-64 builds of the same two modules stay
    `asm:directive/p2align`.

## The census

| Census | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| AArch64 (`census_a64.rs`) | 140 of 211 | 143 of 177 |
| x86-64 | 197 of 220 | 197 of 220 (no template refused for alignment) |

The AArch64 changes, row by row:

- The 34 zstd `.p2align` rows were removed. They are x86-64 templates,
  under `#if defined(__GNUC__) && defined(__x86_64__)`.
- upb's row now holds its `.p2align 3` and assembles.
- The three scalar SIMD rows assemble.
- The NEON `fcmeq v8.8h` row is `asm:operand/vector`.

## Not done

zstd's x86-64 `.p2align` templates stay refused. x86-64 relaxes `-O0`
branches and cuts its prologue (§5.6.15), which would move the padding
off its boundary. The `nop` table also depends on `tune-cpu`.
