# WP-216: AArch64 prefetch, and the next mnemonics real templates use

Spec: `spec/asm.md` §16 (and §5.2, §7, §8).

## What was built

- `crates/hod_asm/src/a64/prefetch.rs`: the 24 prefetch-operation names
  and the words of `prfm` (scaled immediate, register, literal) and
  `prfum`; `encode.rs` `prefetch_insn` reads the operands. `hod_mc_a64`
  is unchanged (no prefetch encoder there; code generation needs none).
- `encode.rs`: `hlt #imm16`, `xpaclri`, and `bic`/`bics`/`orn`/`eon` of
  an immediate (found by the census: mimalloc's `bic x, x, #7`,
  valgrind's `bic sp, x21, #15` in every `CALL_FN_*`).

## The LLVM 19 / 22 difference

`prfm <op>, [xn, #imm]` with an offset the scaled form does not take
(negative, or not a multiple of 8) but the signed 9-bit form does:
`llvm-mc` 22 writes `prfum`; `llvm-mc` 19 rejects the text ("index must
be a multiple of 8 in range [0, 32760]"). hod writes 22's word (kiln's
LLVM). `tests/llvm_mc.rs` `unscaled_prfm_is_llvm_22_s_prfum` checks both
facts. The 24 names (including the `slc` ones) and `#0..#31` are the
same in both; neither knows another name (`ir` and `pldl4keep` are
errors to both).

## Evidence

- `cargo test -q -p hod_asm --test llvm_mc`: 406 bodies, 419 words equal
  `llvm-mc-22`'s and `llvm-mc-19`'s (75 new prefetch, `hlt`, `xpaclri`
  and logical-immediate bodies among them), 6 more `prfm`-as-`prfum`
  bodies against `llvm-mc-22` alone; 28 new refused bodies by name.
- `cargo test -q -p hod_a64 --test inline_asm`: `prefetch_sum` (RocksDB's
  `PRFM PLDL1KEEP, [${0:x},$1]` with `r,I`; register, `sxtw #3`, `uxtw`,
  `prfum`, `#32760`, literal backward and forward) and `xpaclri_lr`,
  built by hod (plain, stress, far) and `llc` 19 and 22, linked with the
  driver under bfd, lld and mold, dynamic and static, and run natively:
  every check passes. `prefetch_words_appear_in_the_code`: the words of a
  fixed-register prefetch template are `llvm-mc` 22's.
- The motivating module
  (`.build/findings/oss3/rocksdb/asm-mnemonic-prfm-12fbb08ba54a.ll`,
  `util/crc32c_arm64.cc`): `hod --strict -O0 -filetype=obj
  -relocation-model=pic` exits 0. In `_Z12crc32c_arm64jPKhm`, hod's
  `.text` holds the 16 prefetches `llc -O0 -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0`
  19 and 22 write: `prfm pldl1keep, [xN, #0x400]` through `#0x7c0` in
  steps of `0x40`, the same operation and offsets in the same order;
  the base is x9 in hod's (`f9820120`..`f983e120`) and x8 in `llc`'s
  (`f9820100`..`f983e100`), the allocator's choice. (`llc` 19 reads the
  module only with kiln's `"frame-pointer"="non-leaf-no-reserve"`
  changed to `"non-leaf"`, an LLVM 22 attribute value.)

## The census

Taken over every `asm(...)` statement of `.build/oss/src`,
`.build/oss2/src`, `.build/oss3/src` and `/usr/include` (317 files,
1742 statements), and the templates of the saved kiln-built modules of
`.build/findings`: the template strings joined, `%N`/`%wN`/`%xN`/
`%[name]` substituted as `llc` prints them (a `Q`/`m` operand as
`[xN]`), and kept when `llvm-mc-22 --triple=aarch64` reads the body (222
bodies; the rest are other architectures). The AArch64 templates among
them (comments, x86 `.byte` sequences read by `llvm-mc` and generic
directives set aside), and the NEON and directive templates `llvm-mc`
could not read only because of the substitution, are the 50 rows of
`crates/hod_asm/tests/census_a64.rs`, 211 templates: **140 assemble**
(116 before this package), each `llvm-mc` 22's and 19's words.
Neither the kernel's arm64 UAPI headers nor glibc's other headers have
an AArch64 template; musl is not installed.
